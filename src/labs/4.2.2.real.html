About this edition

This edition is for doing the lab on your own Android phone or tablet with TalkBack. TalkBack does not use drag and drop for most of these tasks. Instead, it offers an Actions menu on each app icon with choices such as Add to Home screen, Move, Create folder, Remove, and Uninstall. The steps below use that menu.

Your home screen will change during this lab. You can undo everything at the end: remove the shortcuts and widget you add, and delete the folder by moving its apps out. LastPass is free; you do not need to create an account, and you uninstall it in the last step.

Gestures used in this lab

  • Move to the next or previous item: swipe right or left.
  • Activate: double-tap.
  • Touch and hold: double-tap and hold (keep your finger down after the second tap).
  • Actions menu for the item you are on: open the TalkBack menu (swipe down then right, or tap with three fingers on phones that support it) and choose Actions. On some versions, swipe up then right opens Actions directly.
  • Home: swipe up then left. Back: swipe down then left.
  • Next home screen: scroll with two fingers, swiping left.

Menus and names differ by phone maker (Samsung, Google Pixel, and others) and Android version. If a choice is missing, look for a similar name, or ask your instructor.

Part 1: Apps and widgets

Add an app shortcut

  1. Unlock the phone. On the home screen, open the list of all apps: swipe up with two fingers from the bottom, or find the All apps or Apps button.
  2. Move to Calculator. Open the Actions menu and choose Add to Home screen. (On Samsung, double-tap and hold, then choose Add to Home.)
  3. Go home and swipe through the home screen to find the new Calculator shortcut.

Add a widget

  1. On the home screen, find an empty spot by touch, or move to any app icon, then double-tap and hold the home screen background. A menu appears with Widgets. TalkBack may also offer Home screen options in the Actions menu.
  2. Double-tap Widgets. Widgets are grouped by app. Choose one, such as the Clock app's digital clock.
  3. Open the Actions menu on the widget and choose Add to Home screen. TalkBack announces where it was placed.

Move an app to another screen

  1. Move to an app shortcut on the home screen. Open the Actions menu and choose Move or Move to position. On Pixel phones TalkBack then lets you choose a position or another screen; follow its spoken prompts.

Remove a shortcut

  1. Move to the Drive shortcut (or any shortcut you added). Open the Actions menu and choose Remove (Samsung: Remove from Home).
  2. Open the all apps list again and check that Drive is still there. Removing a shortcut does not uninstall the app.

Part 2: Managing folders

  1. Make sure two apps, such as Calculator and Photos, are on the same home screen.
  2. Move to Calculator. Open the Actions menu and choose Create folder with Photos. (On Samsung, choose Select, select both apps, then Create folder.)
  3. Double-tap the folder to open it. Move to the folder name field, double-tap to edit, delete the old name, and type ITE Apps Folder. Close the keyboard and go Back to close the folder.
  4. Open the folder, move to Photos, open the Actions menu, and choose Move out of folder or Remove from folder.

After Photos is moved out of the folder, what happens to the folder and to Calculator?

The folder disappears, because a folder needs at least two apps. Calculator goes back on the home screen where the folder was. (Some Samsung versions keep a folder with one app; if yours does, note that.)

Part 3: Managing apps

  1. Open Play Store. Move to the search field at the top, double-tap, type LastPass, and press the search key on the keyboard.
  2. Move to LastPass Password Manager and double-tap to open its details.
  3. Move to Install and double-tap. TalkBack announces progress; when the button changes to Open, it is installed.
  4. Go home and open the all apps list. Move to LastPass, open the Actions menu, and choose Uninstall. (Or double-tap and hold, then choose Uninstall.)
  5. Confirm with OK or Uninstall.

What is the difference between removing an app from the home screen and uninstalling it?

Removing takes away only the shortcut; the app stays installed. Uninstalling deletes the app and its data from the phone.

Corrections from the original lab

  • The original says widgets "can be installed directly from a screen." Widgets come with installed apps; you add them to a screen.
  • The original targets Android 7.0 and drag gestures. Current Android and TalkBack offer Remove, Uninstall, and folder actions in menus, which this edition uses.
  • Play Store no longer shows an App Permissions screen with Accept during install.
