About this edition

This edition is for doing the lab on your own iPhone or iPad with VoiceOver. VoiceOver users rarely drag icons. Instead, VoiceOver offers actions on each icon (move, create folder, remove), which you hear by swiping up or down. The steps below use those actions. Names can differ a little between iOS versions; listen for the closest match.

Your Home Screen will change during this lab. You can put things back at the end. You need an Apple Account to install the free LastPass app; you do not need to create a LastPass account, and you delete the app in the last step.

Gestures used in this lab

  • Move between items: swipe right or left. Activate: double-tap.
  • Hear an item's actions: swipe down or up while on it (the Actions rotor), then double-tap the action you want.
  • Touch and hold: double-tap and hold, which opens an icon's menu.
  • Next or previous Home Screen page: swipe left or right with three fingers.
  • Go Home: on Face ID iPhones, slide one finger up from the bottom edge until you feel the first vibration; on others, press the Home button.

Part 1: Apps and the Home Screen

  1. Unlock the iPhone and go Home. Move to Contacts (if it is in a folder, such as Utilities, open the folder first, or pick any other app).
  2. Swipe down to hear the actions and choose Edit mode (or double-tap and hold, then choose Edit Home Screen). The icons are now in edit mode, the "jiggle" mode.
  3. On Contacts, swipe down to Drag and double-tap. Swipe left with three fingers until VoiceOver reads a page you want, or the last page, find a place, and swipe down to a Drop before or Drop after action. On some versions VoiceOver offers moving to a new page directly.
  4. Choose Done at the top right of the screen (or press the Home button) to leave edit mode.

Part 2: Managing folders

  1. Find Stocks and Calendar on the same page (move them together first if needed). Turn on edit mode, move to Stocks, choose Drag, move to Calendar, and choose Create folder with Calendar.

What name did iOS give the new folder?

Answers vary. iOS names the folder after the kind of apps in it, for example Productivity or Finance.

  1. Open the folder (double-tap it). In edit mode, move to the folder name, which is a text field. Double-tap it, remove the old name (double-tap the Clear text button next to it, or use the Delete key until it is gone), and type ITE Folder. Choose Done on the keyboard.
  2. Go Home to close the folder.
  3. Open ITE Folder, move to Calendar, choose Drag, leave the folder (go Home or scrub), and drop Calendar on the Home Screen. Do the same with Stocks.

What happens to the folder?

It disappears once it is empty.

Part 3: Installing apps

  1. Open App Store. Move to the tab bar at the bottom and double-tap Search.
  2. Double-tap the search field, type LastPass, and double-tap Search on the keyboard.
  3. Move to LastPass Password Manager and double-tap to open it. Move to Get and double-tap.
  4. Confirm: VoiceOver says "Double-click to install." Double-click the side button, then Face ID checks you; or enter your Apple Account password if asked.
  5. When the button reads Open, go Home and find LastPass (new apps go to the last page or the App Library).

Part 4: Deleting apps

  1. Move to LastPass. Swipe down to Remove App and double-tap (or double-tap and hold, then choose Remove App).
  2. Choose Delete App, then Delete.

In the Remove dialog, what is the difference between Delete App and Remove from Home Screen?

Remove from Home Screen only hides the icon; the app stays installed in the App Library. Delete App removes the app and its data. A few built-in apps, such as Settings and Phone, cannot be deleted at all.

Corrections from the original lab

  • Since iOS 10, most built-in apps can be deleted, and current iOS shows a minus sign, not an X.
  • Installing no longer uses "Get, then Install"; you confirm with the side button, Face ID, Touch ID, or your password.
  • The original relies on drag gestures and unlabeled screenshots. This edition uses VoiceOver actions.
