About this lab

In this lab you rearrange apps on an iPhone Home Screen and move them between pages, create, rename, and empty a folder, and install and delete an app.

The simulated iPhone runs a current version of iOS. On a real iPhone you touch and hold an icon until the icons jiggle, then drag. Here, every icon has an Actions button instead, the same idea as the VoiceOver Actions rotor. Apps fill each page in order, 4 per row. The Apple Account password for this lab is Apple2026!.

Prefer to practice on your own iPhone with VoiceOver? Use the VoiceOver edition of this lab, listed on the hub.

Part 1: Apps and the Home Screen

Step 1.1: Apps and the Home Screen

  1. The simulated iPhone is already unlocked. On a real iPhone, unlock it with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.
  2. Find Contacts on the Home Screen and choose its Actions button. Notice the choices: you can move the app, put it in a folder, or remove it.
  3. Choose Move to another page or position, then New page, and choose Move. iOS creates a second Home Screen page for it. On a real iPhone you drag the icon to the right edge of the screen until the next page slides in, then tap Done (or press the Home button on older iPhones).

Part 2: Managing folders

Step 2.1: Create a folder

  1. Go back to page 1. Choose the Actions button for Stocks, then Create folder with Calendar. On a real iPhone you drag Stocks on top of Calendar.

What name did iOS give the new folder?

Answers vary. iOS names a new folder after the kind of apps in it. In the simulation it is Productivity, the category of Calendar.

Step 2.2: Rename the folder

  1. Open the folder.
  2. In Folder name, delete the name, type ITE Folder, and choose Save name.
  3. Go Home to close the folder.

Step 2.3: Remove apps from the folder

  1. Open ITE Folder. Choose the Actions button for Calendar, then Move out of folder to the Home Screen.
  2. Open ITE Folder again and move Stocks out the same way.

What happens to the folder?

It disappears from the Home Screen once it is empty. (Unlike Android, an iPhone folder stays as long as it holds even one app.)

Part 3: Installing apps

Step 3.1: Installing apps

  1. Open App Store and choose the Search tab.
  2. Search for LastPass, a free password manager, and choose LastPass Password Manager to see its details.
  3. Choose Get. Confirm with your Apple Account password (on a real iPhone, Face ID, Touch ID, or a double-click of the side button may be used instead). Choose Install.
  4. When the button changes to Open, go Home and find LastPass on the last Home Screen page.

You need an Apple Account to install apps on an iPhone, even free ones.

Part 4: Deleting apps

Step 4.1: Deleting apps

  1. Choose the Actions button for LastPass, then Remove App.
  2. Choose Delete App, then confirm with Delete.

In the Remove dialog, what is the difference between Delete App and Remove from Home Screen? Which apps cannot be deleted?

Remove from Home Screen only hides the icon; the app stays installed in the App Library. Delete App removes the app and its data. A few built-in apps, such as Settings, Phone, Messages, Camera, Photos, Safari, and App Store, cannot be deleted, only removed from the Home Screen. Most other built-in apps, such as Stocks and Calendar, can be deleted and reinstalled from the App Store.

Corrections from the original lab

  • The original says "Apps without the X are default apps and cannot be uninstalled." Since iOS 10, most built-in apps can be deleted. Current iOS also shows a minus sign, not an X, and offers Delete App or Remove from Home Screen.
  • The original says "Touch Get and then touch Install." Current iOS confirms with Face ID, Touch ID, a side button double-click, or the Apple Account password.
  • The original's iOS 10 instructions rely on the Home button. iPhones without one use Done at the top of the screen.
  • The original's nine screenshots have no text descriptions. Every step here is written out.
  • Question 3 is added to make the difference between removing and deleting explicit.
