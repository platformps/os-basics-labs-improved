About this lab

In this lab you turn the Wi-Fi radio off and on, forget a saved Wi-Fi network, and find and join it again, first on an Android phone and then on an iPhone.

The simulation has two phones: a Samsung Galaxy phone running Android and an iPhone running iOS. Choose the phone with the Phone buttons at the top of the simulation. Both are near the home network HomeNet. The HomeNet password is NetLab2026!.

Prefer to practice on your own phone with VoiceOver or TalkBack? Use the screen reader edition of this lab, listed on the hub. On a real phone, only forget a network whose password you know.

Step 1: Configure Wi-Fi on an Android device

  1. Choose the Android phone. On a real phone you would unlock it first with your PIN, password, or pattern.
  2. Open All apps, then Settings, then Connections.
  3. Turn the Wi-Fi switch off.
  4. Turn the Wi-Fi switch on again. Wait until the phone says it is connected to HomeNet.
  5. Choose Wi-Fi to open the Wi-Fi screen, then choose HomeNet under Current network. The network details screen opens.
  6. Choose Forget.

What happens?

The phone disconnects. HomeNet still appears in the list of available networks, because it is still in range, but the phone is not connected and no longer has the password saved.

  1. Choose HomeNet in Available networks.
  2. Type the Wi-Fi password and choose Connect.

What happens?

The phone connects to HomeNet and saves the network again. If the password is wrong, the phone reports an authentication error and does not connect.

Step 2: Configure Wi-Fi on an iOS device

  1. Choose the iPhone.
  2. Open Settings, then Wi-Fi.
  3. Turn the Wi-Fi switch off, then on again. Wait until HomeNet shows as connected.
  4. Choose More info (the i button) next to HomeNet. Its details screen opens.
  5. Choose Forget This Network, then confirm with Forget.

What happens?

The iPhone disconnects and moves HomeNet from the connected spot to the Networks list. It is still in range, but the iPhone is not connected and will not join it automatically.

  1. Choose HomeNet in the Networks list.
  2. Type the Wi-Fi password and choose Join.

What happens?

The iPhone joins HomeNet and shows it as connected, with a checkmark.

Corrections from the original lab

  • The original page title is the placeholder "New Block Title," so a screen reader announces that instead of the lab name.
  • The Android path All apps, Settings, Connections is the Samsung path. On a Google Pixel and most other Android phones it is Settings, Network and internet, Internet. The simulation follows the Samsung path from the lab; the screen reader edition gives both.
  • On an iPhone, choosing the name of the network you are already connected to does nothing. The details open from the More info (i) button next to the name.
  • The original has the typo "the network to which the device should connect to."
