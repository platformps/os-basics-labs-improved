    About this edition

    This edition is for doing the lab on your own phone with its built-in screen reader: TalkBack on Android or VoiceOver on iPhone. The steps name the gestures to use. You only need one phone; do the part that matches it. The questions and answers are the same as in the simulation edition.

    Before you start: make sure you know your Wi-Fi password. You will make the phone forget the network, and you need the password to join again. If you are not sure of it, use a phone hotspot or a network whose password is posted, or use the simulation edition instead. TalkBack and VoiceOver keep working with Wi-Fi off.

    Gestures used in this lab

    • Move to the next or previous item: swipe right or left with one finger (both screen readers).
    • Activate the item you are on: double-tap anywhere (both).
    • Go back: TalkBack, swipe down then left, or use the Back button. VoiceOver, scrub with two fingers in a Z shape, or find the Back button at the top left.
    • Go home: TalkBack, swipe up then left. VoiceOver, on Face ID iPhones, slide one finger up from the bottom edge until you feel the first vibration, then lift; on Home button iPhones, press the Home button.
    • Type: double-tap a text field to start editing, then type on the on-screen keyboard (move to a key and double-tap, or lift your finger with touch typing) or on a Bluetooth keyboard.

    Gestures can differ slightly by TalkBack version, phone maker, and VoiceOver settings. If a gesture does nothing, check your screen reader's gesture settings.

    Part A: Android with TalkBack

    Turn on TalkBack if it is not already on: Settings, Accessibility, TalkBack. Unlock the phone.

    Find the Wi-Fi settings

    1. Open Settings: swipe up from the home screen to open the apps list (or find All apps), move to Settings, and double-tap.
    2. Samsung Galaxy: move to Connections and double-tap, then move to Wi-Fi. Google Pixel and most other phones: move to Network and internet, double-tap, then Internet.

    Turn Wi-Fi off and on

    1. Move to the Wi-Fi switch. TalkBack says "Wi-Fi, on, switch." Double-tap. It says "off."
    2. Double-tap again to turn it on. Wait a few seconds, then swipe through the screen until you hear your network name followed by "Connected."

    Forget the network

    1. Samsung: double-tap Wi-Fi (the words, not the switch) to open the Wi-Fi screen, then move to your network under Current network. Find its Settings button (TalkBack may read it as "Settings" or "Network details") and double-tap. Pixel: move to your network and double-tap the Settings button just after it.
    2. Move to Forget and double-tap.

    What happens?

    The phone disconnects. The network is still in the list of available networks because it is in range, but the phone is no longer connected and no longer has the password saved.

    Join the network again

    1. Move to your network in the list and double-tap.
    2. Double-tap the Password field and type the password. TalkBack speaks password characters only if you turned on speaking passwords in its settings.
    3. Move to Connect and double-tap.

    What happens?

    The phone connects and saves the network. TalkBack reads the network name followed by "Connected." A wrong password gives an authentication error.

    Part B: iPhone with VoiceOver

    Turn on VoiceOver if it is not already on: Settings, Accessibility, VoiceOver, or ask Siri to turn on VoiceOver. Unlock the iPhone.

    1. On the Home Screen, move to Settings and double-tap. (Or ask Siri to open Settings.)
    2. Move to Wi-Fi and double-tap.
    3. Move to the Wi-Fi switch. VoiceOver says "Wi-Fi, switch button, on." Double-tap to turn it off, then double-tap again to turn it on. Wait until VoiceOver reads your network as "selected" or "connected."
    4. Swipe right from your network name to its More info button and double-tap.
    5. Move to Forget This Network and double-tap. In the alert, move to Forget and double-tap.

    What happens?

    The iPhone disconnects and lists the network under Networks. It is still in range, but the iPhone is not connected and will not join it on its own.

    1. Move to your network under Networks and double-tap.
    2. Focus starts in the Password field. Type the password.
    3. Move to Join (at the top right; it is dimmed until the password is long enough) and double-tap.

    What happens?

    The iPhone joins the network. VoiceOver reads it as selected or connected, and Wi-Fi in Settings shows the network name.
