    03About the editions

    Accessible simulation

    A realistic copy of the computer, tools, or phone, running in your browser. Nothing to install. Works with a keyboard and screen readers, at any zoom level, in light or dark mode. The checklist marks itself as you go.

    Screen reader edition for your own phone

    For the phone labs. You do the lab on your own phone with VoiceOver (iPhone) or TalkBack (Android), with steps written for screen reader gestures instead of looking and tapping.

    Original Cisco lab

    The lab as written in your NetAcad course. Several original labs need a virtual machine or a phone and rely on screenshots and mouse actions, so they may not work with assistive technology.

    04Getting around and getting help

    • With a screen reader, press H to move from heading to heading. Modules are level 2 headings and labs are level 3 headings, so the 3 key jumps from lab to lab.
    • Each lab opens in this same tab. Use the "All labs" button at the top of a lab to come back here.
    • Inside a simulation, your answers are saved as you type. Use "Download my answers" to save a text file you can send to your instructor.
    • If anything in a lab does not work with your setup, tell your instructor which lab, which step, and what you use (for example NVDA with Chrome). Each report helps us fix it for everyone.
